    Methodology & trust

    What AssemblyEdge measures, where production data comes from, and what this prototype is not.

    Sources (production)

    • CAL-ACCESS — candidate & IE filings, late contributions
    • Meta Ad Library — creatives, spend bands, dates
    • Google Ads Transparency — creatives, spend bands
    • News — sourced headlines with outlet + timestamp

    Money tab loads LIVE CAL-ACCESS from data/calaccess/latest/money-by-district.json when present (as-of stamped). Otherwise demo placeholders — never silently mixed. See CALACCESS.md.

    Update cadence (target)

    • Ad libraries: multiple times daily
    • CAL-ACCESS deltas: on filing / late-contribution cycles
    • Narrative: continuous ingest with human-reviewable tags
    • Monday Brief: weekly rollup for operators

    Threat Index factors

    • Money velocity — receipts/spend vs 8-week baseline
    • IE pressure — independent expenditure concentration
    • Ad surge — Meta + Google volume & bands
    • Narrative heat — headline velocity + sentiment
    • Poll movement — disclosed ranges only when available
    • Polling & gaps panel — curated data/polling/latest.json (public horse-race only)
    • Focus map — 40% TI + 35% money + 25% polling gap severity (prototype)

    Click any factor on AD-7 for an in-product methodology drawer.

    Decision chips

    Alerts surface a decision type — Consider spend / Hold & monitor / Message check — as operator guidance taxonomy. They are not automated orders, win probabilities, or legal advice. CM Board extends this with Field surge and a persisted decision log.

    Campaign Manager doctrine (attributions)

    • Issenberg, The Victory Lab — measure & test what moves votes
    • Green & Gerber, Get Out the Vote — evidence-backed GOTV tactics
    • Obama 2012 analytics (public postmortems) — actionable real-time ops loops
    • Romney ORCA (public postmortems) — ED systems need tested fallbacks
    • Standard CM craft — huddle, burn-rate, mode switch, oppo, RR, ballot chase, BOE
    • Field capacity — ground-war plans constrained by staff/volunteer capacity

    Citations are short attributions only — no copyrighted excerpts. See PRODUCT.md Doctrine map.
