Channel Ops

Internet & marketplace listing implementation · Not legal advice

If you sell passenger or off-highway motor vehicle parts to California buyers online, a posted 5×5 sign at your counter usually does not replace the internet warning methods in 27 CCR § 25602. Listings and product pages need their own clear WARNING path before purchase — eBay descriptions, Amazon warning fields, Shopify product pages, and similar channels each have different placement rules.

What Channel Ops includes

Paid download pack (not offered as a free public zip on this site):

  • eBay listing HTML with the published § 25607.51 safe-harbor sentence
  • Amazon WARNING / hyperlink field notes (anchor text limited to WARNING, CA WARNING, or CALIFORNIA WARNING)
  • Shopify / WooCommerce product-page snippet
  • Catalog / feed blurb text
  • Placement checklist for marketplace and mobile layouts

Source files live in the repository under docs/assets/channel-ops/ for product bundling — they ship to customers as part of paid fulfillment, not as an open download here.

Official safe-harbor text (27 CCR § 25607.51) Handling passenger or off-highway motor vehicle parts can expose you to chemicals such as phthalates and lead, which can cause cancer and reproductive harm. To minimize exposure, service the vehicle in a well-ventilated area, wear gloves, and wash your hands. For more information see www.P65Warnings.ca.gov/motor-vehicle-parts.
