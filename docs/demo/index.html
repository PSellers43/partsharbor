Shop Kit demo

Visual walkthrough of the $29 digital download

A step-by-step look at the print-ready Prop 65 files included in the PartsHarbor Shop Kit. Open the zip, print the PDFs, peel the labels, and use the listing files — no subscription required.

1

PartsHarbor Shop Kit

One zip file delivered at purchase. Contains print-ready PDFs, label sheets, marketplace listing files, and a README with printing notes.

2

5×5 counter sign PDF

Square counter sign for your parts desk or service bay. 20-pt type, yellow 25603(a)(1) warning symbol, and the exact 27 CCR § 25607.51 English safe-harbor sentence. Print at any print shop or on an office poster printer.

3

English labels sheet

Avery-style adhesive label layout — 30 labels per sheet. Same official safe-harbor text, sized for product bins, shelves, or individual part packaging at the counter.

4

Spanish labels sheet

Optional OEHHA-published Spanish translation on the same Avery-style grid. Includes the Spanish-language fact sheet URL required for Spanish warnings.

5

Manufacturer→retailer written notice

Fillable PDF template under 27 CCR § 25600.2 for manufacturers, producers, or distributors providing warning materials to retail shops. Includes acknowledgment section — page 1 of 2 in the kit file.

6

Listing files and README

Text-based files for online sales channels — no preview image, included in the zip:

  • eBay listing HTML — paste into your listing description
  • Amazon WARNING-hyperlink notes — guidance for marketplace product warnings under 27 CCR § 25602
  • README — file guide, printing tips, and scope notes (25607.50(b) exclusions)
