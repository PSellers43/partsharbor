Practical note for parts sellers (not a legal conclusion)

California’s tailored safe-harbor methods for motor vehicle parts (including posted 5×5 / internet listing methods under the published CCR text) are about how warnings are presented, not about PartsHarbor deciding whether your SKU needs a warning. If you sell parts into CA and want print-ready / paste-ready files, see the demo and Shop Kit.

Official safe-harbor text (27 CCR § 25607.51) Handling passenger or off-highway motor vehicle parts can expose you to chemicals such as phthalates and lead, which can cause cancer and reproductive harm. To minimize exposure, service the vehicle in a well-ventilated area, wear gloves, and wash your hands. For more information see www.P65Warnings.ca.gov/motor-vehicle-parts.
