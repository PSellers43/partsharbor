For sellers of passenger and off-highway motor vehicle parts who ship into California. Each issue summarizes what showed up in the California Attorney General’s Prop 65 60-day notice search for the prior calendar week.

Monday CA AG watch

PartsHarbor checks the OAG 60-day notice search on a Monday rhythm and publishes a short digest either way — including weeks with zero parts-relevant notices. Quiet weeks are still useful as a baseline so you can skim one place instead of digging the database every week.

We filter for notices that appear relevant to passenger / OHV / motorcycle / ATV-UTV / salvage motor-vehicle parts. Dominant themes in the broader notice set (food, cosmetics, apparel, etc.) are noted when they are not parts-related. This is factual reporting, not a legal conclusion about your SKUs.
