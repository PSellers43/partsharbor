Prop 65 on eBay listings vs packing slips

Motor-vehicle parts · California buyers · Not legal advice

If you sell passenger or off-highway motor vehicle parts on eBay (or similar marketplaces) and ship to California, you have probably seen forum threads about “Prop 65 eBay listing” requirements versus tucking a warning into the packing slip or a loose package insert. This guide is educational ops context for that question — it does not decide whether your SKU needs a warning at all.

Two different moments in the buyer journey

A marketplace sale has at least two visible touchpoints: what the buyer reads before they pay, and what arrives after the order ships. California’s published safe-harbor framework for internet sales is built around the pre-purchase path. OEHHA’s internet warning methods live in 27 CCR § 25602 (see also the motor-vehicle-parts hub at p65warnings.ca.gov/motor-vehicle-parts).

By contrast, a packing slip, invoice PDF, or thank-you card in the box is usually seen after checkout. Treating that paperwork as your only warning for online CA buyers is a common gap in channel planning — not because slips are useless for other business purposes, but because they are a different channel from the listing buyers shop on.

What “before purchase” tends to mean on eBay

For tailored motor-vehicle-parts warnings, the published safe-harbor content is the sentence in 27 CCR § 25607.51. The method on the internet is one of the options in § 25602(b)(1) — for example displaying that sentence on the product display page, using a clearly marked hyperlink with anchor text limited to WARNING / CA WARNING / CALIFORNIA WARNING, or other listed internet methods.

On eBay, that usually translates to practical tasks your listing team already understands:

  • Put the § 25607.51 sentence (or a compliant hyperlink destination) in the item description or another field the buyer sees while evaluating the listing — not only in a store-wide theme footer.
  • Check mobile layout: if the warning is dozens of screen-scrolls below photos and price, treat that as a placement review item (see our Channel Ops placement themes).
  • Keep marketplace-specific fields filled — Amazon WARNING fields and eBay HTML blocks are separate from whatever you print for the warehouse.

PartsHarbor’s free warning channels checklist walks the same split: section 2 is online/marketplace; section 3 is labels/packaging.

Where packing slips and inserts still fit

Warehouse teams often add Prop 65 language to slips for internal consistency, retailer handoffs, or label-adjacent workflows. That can support ops clarity, but it should not be confused with replacing the internet method for the same SKU sold online into California. When you also sell from a physical counter, § 25607.50(a)(3)(B) describes needing both a posted warning method and internet methods under § 25602(b) when the product is sold on the internet — a posted sign alone does not cover your eBay channel.

On-product or in-box labels (Avery-style sheets, etc.) are yet another channel. They may help in-person or fulfillment workflows, but listing-level methods still need their own review for internet orders.

Scope reminder (passenger / OHV parts)

The tailored motor-vehicle-parts safe harbor in § 25607.51 does not cover every automotive-adjacent SKU. Site scope notes mirror 27 CCR § 25607.50(b) exclusions such as tires, packaged service chemicals, asbestos parts, carpeting, upholstery, textiles, or fabrics. If you are outside that parts lane, different OEHHA categories apply — start from official pages rather than parts-specific templates.

Official safe-harbor text (27 CCR § 25607.51) Handling passenger or off-highway motor vehicle parts can expose you to chemicals such as phthalates and lead, which can cause cancer and reproductive harm. To minimize exposure, service the vehicle in a well-ventilated area, wear gloves, and wash your hands. For more information see www.P65Warnings.ca.gov/motor-vehicle-parts.

FAQ

Does a Prop 65 warning on an eBay packing slip replace the listing warning?

For internet sales into California, published internet methods under § 25602 are aimed at the pre-purchase product display path. Post-checkout slips are a different channel and are generally not treated as a substitute for listing-level warnings in ops planning for motor-vehicle parts.

Where should the safe-harbor text appear on eBay?

Use a § 25602(b) internet method so CA buyers can reach the § 25607.51 sentence (or compliant WARNING hyperlink) before purchase — commonly the item description HTML field.

Can an in-box label replace the description warning?

Labels and listings are separate channels. Internet orders still need internet methods even if you also label boxes or run a counter sign in California.
