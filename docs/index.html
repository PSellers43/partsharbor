What it is

The PartsHarbor Shop Kit is a one-time digital download of original print layouts using OEHHA and California Code of Regulations public warning text.

01

Print-ready files

PDFs, label sheets, listing snippets, and a README — ready to print and use at your counter or in online listings.

02

Official safe-harbor text

Warning language follows 27 CCR § 25607.51 (operative January 1, 2025), including the 25603(a)(1) symbol and the word WARNING.

03

Original layouts

Layouts are original — not a licensed dataset. Posted signs use the published safe-harbor sentence; chemical names cannot be swapped on posted signs.

Official safe-harbor text (27 CCR § 25607.51) Handling passenger or off-highway motor vehicle parts can expose you to chemicals such as phthalates and lead, which can cause cancer and reproductive harm. To minimize exposure, service the vehicle in a well-ventilated area, wear gloves, and wash your hands. For more information see www.P65Warnings.ca.gov/motor-vehicle-parts.

Who it's for

Sellers of passenger or off-highway motor vehicle parts who ship or sell into California and need print-ready Prop 65 warning materials.

eBay sellers Amazon sellers Parts counters Small distributors
Scope limits This kit does not apply to tires, packaged service chemicals, asbestos parts, carpeting, upholstery, textiles, or fabrics (27 CCR § 25607.50(b)). Internet sales also require methods under 27 CCR § 25602 in addition to posted or product warnings.
