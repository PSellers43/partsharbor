What it is

The PartsHarbor Shop Kit is a one-time digital download: original print layouts using OEHHA and California Code of Regulations public warning text. You receive files at purchase — PDFs, label sheets, listing snippets, and a README — ready to print and use at your parts counter or in your online listings.

Warning language follows the official safe-harbor text at 27 CCR § 25607.51 (operative January 1, 2025), including the 25603(a)(1) symbol and the word WARNING:

Official safe-harbor text (27 CCR § 25607.51) Handling passenger or off-highway motor vehicle parts can expose you to chemicals such as phthalates and lead, which can cause cancer and reproductive harm. To minimize exposure, service the vehicle in a well-ventilated area, wear gloves, and wash your hands. For more information see www.P65Warnings.ca.gov/motor-vehicle-parts.

Layouts are original. This is not a licensed dataset. Posted signs use the published safe-harbor sentence — chemical names cannot be swapped on posted signs.

Who it's for

Sellers of passenger or off-highway motor vehicle parts who ship or sell into California and need print-ready Prop 65 warning materials:

eBay sellers Amazon sellers Parts counters Small distributors
Scope limits This kit does not apply to tires, packaged service chemicals, asbestos parts, carpeting, upholstery, textiles, or fabrics (27 CCR § 25607.50(b)). Internet sales also require methods under 27 CCR § 25602 in addition to posted or product warnings.

What's in the zip

  • 5×5 counter sign PDF — 20-pt type, yellow triangle, exact 27 CCR § 25607.51 English sentence
  • English labels sheet — Avery-style adhesive label layout

How fulfillment works

  1. Purchase the Shop Kit — one-time $29 payment for the digital download (when the store launches).

  3. Print and use — open PDFs in any print shop or office printer; peel labels from Avery-style sheets; paste eBay HTML or follow Amazon notes for online listings.
  4. Optional updates — if OEHHA changes the published warning text, you may add the optional Warning Updates file pack ($12/month) for revised files. This is optional file updates, not a service or membership.
