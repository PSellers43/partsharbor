Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PartsHarbor ("we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website, join our waitlist, or purchase digital products from us.

Who we are

PartsHarbor is operated by Patrick Sellers. Contact: patrick.ferrparts@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Contact information — If you email us or join the waitlist, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message.
  • Purchase information — When you buy a digital product, our payment and fulfillment provider (Lemon Squeezy) collects your name, email address, and payment details. We receive your name and email to deliver your download and provide support.
  • Website usage — This static site does not use analytics cookies or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages may log standard server access data (IP address, browser type, pages viewed) as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

How we use information

  • Deliver digital downloads and optional file updates
  • Respond to support requests
  • Notify waitlist subscribers when the store launches
  • Process refunds when applicable under our Refund Policy

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Lemon Squeezy — payment processing and digital product delivery
  • Service providers — only as needed to operate the site or fulfill orders
  • Legal requirements — if required by law or to protect our rights

Data retention

We retain purchase and support records as long as needed to fulfill orders, handle refunds, and maintain business records. You may request deletion of waitlist or support correspondence by emailing us.

Your choices

You may opt out of waitlist emails at any time by replying to any message or contacting us directly. California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA; contact us to exercise them.

Children

Our products are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect changes.

Contact

Questions about this policy: patrick.ferrparts@gmail.com
